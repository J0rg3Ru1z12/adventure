## path-1
You arriave at Inconvenience city's zoo, where a Massive banner hangs over the entrance:

"WELCOME TO QUACKNILIOUS CHARLSON III'S BREADDOM. NO OUTSIDE FOOD. ALL FOOD IN HERE IS ALSO MINE!!!"

Looking further beyond, the mayor sits in a giant cage besides the breadcovered pond. Quacknilious Charlson III practically towers over him. laughing maniacally, which only sounds like a rusted car engine.

Two breaded up ducks guard the main entrance. Nearby, a nervous zookeeper waves ovver at you and points to a maintenance shed.

[Talk to the zookeeper](#zoo-shed)
[Approach the duck guards](#duck-checkpoint)
[Sneak toward the mayor's cage](#mayor-cage)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## zoo-shed
The zookeeper pulls you in and locks the door.

"Finally! A superhero!" she whispers.

You introduce Yourself.

She pauses. The glimmer of hope going down into a deadpan smile.

"Uhh.... right. Well... I guess we're greatful somebody showed up."

She holds the bag of soggy bread to you.

[take the soggy bread](#soggy-bread)
[tell her you don't need a perfectly convenient item](#tell-her-off)

## soggy-bread

"The guards can be distracted by these. They're too hungry to even stop themselves from eating anything. The cage key is hanging from Quacknilious's whistle, but there is a spare in that red emergency box that sits beside the pond."

"How do I open the box?"

"It's unlocked. Just open it."

[head towards the duck guards](#duck-checkpoint)
[Sneak toward the emergency box](#zoo-emergency-box)


## tell-her-off
You reject inconvenient soggy bread to which she gets mad and rats you out to the ducks, throwing 3 perfectly convenient bread at you. You feel poisoned by the convenience of perfectly done bread and die on the spot.

THE END

"FOOD!" one quacks.

While they argue over a crumb, you slip through the gages and reach the red emergency box.

[Open the emergency box](#zoo-emergency-box)

you raise your hands dramatically and summon a very small dissapointing mosquito.

Quacknilious feels the disturbance through his senses of food and comes to you, slapping you across the face and putting you in a cage. 

A humiliating convenient defeat.

"I'm Quacknilious Charlson III's lawyer."

the guards exchange a worried glance and immediately escort you to their boss.

Quacknilious bends down.

"Excellent. Explain the legal difference between stealing bread and collecting taxes."

you open your mouth. 

Nothing comes out.

Quacknilious Charlson III grins widely.

"A real lawyer would have charged me. You are no Mike Ross."

He slaps a piece of perfectly convenient. dry bread, Your kryptonite and fall unconcious. He takes you in and cages you put forever

get: cage-key

[Go to the mayor's cage](#mayor-cage)


## mayor-cage
The mayor grips the bars.

“Thank goodness! Did they send someone powerful?”

“They sent someone available,” you whisper.

The cage door is secured with a heavy padlock. Beside it sits a large metal water trough with a faded label:

“EMERGENCY COOLING BATH.”

Quacknilious's speech grows louder behind you.

You probably have a few seconds before he turns around.

[Unlock the cage with the spare key](#zoo-unlock-cage) if cage-key
[Try to pick the lock with your plastic spoon](#zoo-spoon)
[Retreat and look for a key](#path-1)

## zoo-spoon
You slide your slightly bent plastic spoon into the lock.

For one glorious second, you feel like a professional.

Then the spoon snaps.

The mayor stares at the broken handle.

“Was that your entire plan?”

Before you can explain your backup plan—which was mostly running—Quacknilious's enormous shadow falls over you.

"Well well... Poor performance indeed"

Both you and the mayor look up to see Quacknilious grining widely, probably because he was watching this happen without you or the mayor knowing. Quacknilious then picks up the mayor and throws a convinient piece of bread at you. It lands on your head and immediately you feel yourself weaken.

THUD

You collapse beside your broken spoon

Quacknilious checks the lock on the mayor's cage, then looks down to you with a grin.

"Look at you Captain Inconvenience, down on the ground like a inconvenient hero should be"

He sneers as his guards come in.

"Take Captin Inconvenience to the cellar. Make sure he stays there seated in the most comfortable chair we have. Perfect back support. Everything within arms length."

The guards gasp in horror.

"S-Sir... isn't that a bit too cruel for an Inconvenient hero?"

"Exactly. Make sure as well that there is a convenient parking spot right outside as well with the sun right in front, too."

The guards pick you up and waddle away with you, still with that piece of convenient bread on your head to silence your power forever.

Your last thought before losing consciouseness is that everything was going a bit too conveniently.

THE END

## zoo-unlock-cage
The key turns. The cage door swings open.

The mayor steps out, catches his shoe on your cape, and crashes into the metal water trough.

CLANG.

Quacknilious stops speaking.

He turns slowly toward you.

“NOBODY STEALS MY ELECTED OFFICIAL.”

You glance at the trough. You could try warming its water and bluffing, throw your remaining breadcrumbs, or simply run.

[Warm the water and announce that duck soup is on the menu](#zoo-warm-water)
[Throw breadcrumbs to cause a feeding frenzy](#zoo-bread-escape)
[Run for the zoo exit with the mayor](#zoo-running-escape)

## zoo-warm-water
You plunge one hand into the trough and activate your power.

The cold water becomes slightly warm.

Steam does not rise. Nothing glows. Somewhere, a pigeon looks disappointed.

You stand anyway.

“ONE STEP CLOSER, Quacknilious, AND YOU'RE GETTING A WARM BATH.”

Quacknilious freezes.

“A bath?”

You point at the label.

“Emergency cooling bath.”

Quacknilious backs away.

“That is clearly the opposite of cooling.”

he throws a piece of bread at you and you fall unconcious

THE END


## zoo-bread-escape
You fling the remaining breadcrumbs toward Quacknilious's feet.

Every duck in the enclosure charges forward.

The guards abandon their weapons. Quacknilious interrupts his own threat to shout, “SAVE SOME FOR ME!”

Within seconds, the criminal empire becomes a furious argument over snacks.

You and the mayor escape through the maintenance gate.

Outside, he promises you a medal, a parade, and reimbursement for the breadcrumbs.

You ask about your broken cape.

“Let's not get unreasonable,” he says.

The mayor is safe, Quacknilious's guards are distracted, and Captain Inconvenience has finally defeated organized crime with catering.

THE END

## zoo-running-escape
You grab the mayor's hand and sprint toward the exit.

Unfortunately, your cape catches on the cage door.

The guards return you both to the cage. This time, Quack confiscates your cape.

The mayor calls that an improvement.

THE END
