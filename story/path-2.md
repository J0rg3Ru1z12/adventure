## path-2
You chase the delivery truck for six blocks. It stops at every red light, every yellow light, and once for a pigeon. For the first time in your career, you are the one being inconvenienced.

The truck finally turns into an alley and parks behind a warehouse. A hand-painted sign reads: **TOTALLY NORMAL BAKERY SUPPLY CO. (NOT DUCKS)**.

The trench coat climbs out, wobbles, and splits into three ducks. They waddle inside carrying crates of stolen bread.

[Knock on the front door](#warehouse-front)
[Sneak around to the loading dock](#warehouse-dock)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## warehouse-front
A small window in the door slides open. A duck in reading glasses looks you up and down.

"Password."

You think hard. Every clue you have points to one word.

[Say "bread"](#dumpster-ending)
[Say "please"](#dumpster-ending)
[Back away slowly](#path-2)

## dumpster-ending
"Wrong," says the duck. "The password was *bread, but fancy*."

The door opens just long enough for two security ducks to carry you out by your cape and drop you into the dumpster behind the warehouse.

It is full of stale heel pieces. You have never felt more defeated, or more comfortable.

THE END

## warehouse-dock
The loading dock is dark and smells like sourdough. Nobody is guarding it, because the guard is asleep on a sack of flour.

On a hook by the door hangs a second trench coat with a tag pinned to it: **SPARE DISGUISE. DO NOT WEAR IF HUMAN.**

You put it on. It fits perfectly, which worries you a little.

get: trench coat

[Head deeper into the warehouse](#sorting-floor)
[Go back to the alley](#path-2)

## sorting-floor
Conveyor belts carry thousands of stolen loaves through the warehouse: rye, wheat, brioche, and one sad gluten-free muffin nobody wants.

At the far end stands a steel door marked **LOAF VAULT**. A foreman duck with a hard hat checks everyone who walks through it.

[Waddle past the foreman in your trench coat](#loaf-vault) if trench coat
[Use your power to make the conveyor belts stop every few feet](#conveyor-chaos)
[Sneak back out to the loading dock](#warehouse-dock)

## conveyor-chaos
You concentrate. Every conveyor belt in the warehouse starts stopping and starting like an elevator that visits every floor.

Bread piles up. Ducks blame each other. The foreman blames the union. Then the biggest pile of baguettes tips over, and you disappear under it.

When the ducks finally dig you out, they are so impressed by the mess that they hire you as their new Head of Delays.

The pay is terrible, but the bread is free.

THE END

## loaf-vault
The foreman squints at you. "You look tall for a duck."

"Quack," you say, with confidence.

He waves you through.

Inside the vault, the most valuable bread in the city sits locked inside a giant birdcage, the same kind holding the mayor at the zoo. A clipboard on the wall reads: **SHIPMENT FOR THE MACHINE BENEATH THE CITY. LEAVES TONIGHT.**

[Unlock the cage with the spare birdcage key](#vault-opened) if cage-key
[Steal the delivery truck instead](#truck-escape)
[Slip back out to the sorting floor](#sorting-floor)

## vault-opened
The spare key from the zoo fits the lock perfectly. The cage swings open.

Every duck in the warehouse smells the good bread at the same moment. They abandon the conveyor belts, the foreman, and their careers, and stampede into the vault.

By the time Quacknilious Charlson III hears the news, his entire bread-smuggling ring has eaten itself out of business.

The shipment for the machine never leaves the warehouse. You walk out the front door, still wearing the trench coat, and nobody stops you.

THE END

## truck-escape
You climb into the delivery truck. The three ducks who usually drive it are on their lunch break, which is also bread.

You drive the truck straight to Walmart and unload every crate into the empty bread aisle. The shoppers cheer. One of them cries into a hotdog bun.

The bread is back, but the special shipment is still locked in the vault. Somewhere below the city, the machine keeps humming.

THE END
