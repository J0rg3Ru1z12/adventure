# Captin Inconvenience
by: CS 1101
Collaborators: Jorge Ruiz, Kevin Corpus, jocelyn huerta, Carlos Fuerte
theme: paper

## dock
![A small rocky island at dusk, with a dark lighthouse on the cliff](images/island.svg)

In the year of 2077, Humanity has been subjected into creating artificial intelligence, self driving cars, reuseable rockets, and refrigerators that connect to WIFI for reasons that no one can explain properly.

Despite all of these quirky things going on, civilization still somehow remains completely unprepared for what is coming.... and that is giant Evil animals

three days ago, a mysterious piece of bread fell from space and landed on a pond at the Brown city. An ordinary duck that named Quacknilious Charlson III ate that piece and transformed into a 40 foot tall duck.

Quacknilious took control of the city's ducks, established an underground special bread smuggling ring, and declared himself the ruler of Inconvenience City. 

BUT.... there was only one person who can Save this Inconvenience City 
[Continue] (#heros-call)

The mayor attempted peaceful negotiations armed with a bag of breadcrumbs and an optimistic attitude. He was immediately taken hostage by Quacknilious Charlson III

The military failed. The fridges ran, Bread crumbled AWAY, The local supermarkets closed its bread aisle, sparking widespread panic and three separate arguments over the last package of random hotdog buns

With the city on the brink of collapse, its desperate citizens turned to the only superhero still answering his phone.

Unfortunately, that hero was you: Captain Inconvenience.

Your abilities include turning cold water slightly warm, making elevators stop on every floor, and summoning a single mosquito that you cannot control. Your greatest recorded achievement was lowering a criminal's phone battery to one percent just before an important call.

Even your mother introduces you as “my son, who's still figuring things out.”

Now, armed with a slightly bent plastic spoon and a cape that violates several basic safety standards, you must rescue the mayor, dismantle Quacknilious Charlson III's bread empire, and uncover the truth behind the radioactive loaf.

[Answer the city's call for help](#crossroads)

## Crossroads
You stand in downtown Inconvenience City as Quack Nicholson's voice echoes through the streets.

“YOUR BREAD BELONGS TO ME. YOU MAY KEEP THE HEEL PIECES.”

A delivery truck rolls past, driven by three ducks stacked inside a total normal trench coat. Through its open back doors, you spot crates of stolen bread.

In the distance, Quacknilious towers above the city zoo. The mayor hangs nearby in a giant birdcage, still clutching his bag of breadcrumbs.

Behind you, frightened shoppers pound on Walmart's locked doors. One claims to have seen a loaf of bread glowing green in the closed bread aisle.

Then the sidewalk trembles beneath your feet. Somewhere below the city, a mysterious machine begins to hum.

You straighten your cape and consider your options..
